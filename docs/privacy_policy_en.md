# MartNote Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-05-14**

MartNote ("the App") respects user privacy. This policy describes what information the App collects and how it's used.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App is usable **without any account or sign-in**, and does **not directly collect personally identifiable information**.

The following anonymous information may be automatically collected:

- **Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)**: Anonymous aggregate data about which screens and features are used.
- **Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)**: Anonymous diagnostic information (device model, OS version, stack trace) when the App crashes.
- **Advertising ID (Google AdMob)**: Used by Google AdMob for ad delivery and measurement. Users can reset the ad ID or opt out of personalized ads in device settings.

## 2. Data Storage

All user data — grocery lists, marts, items, preferences — is **stored only on the user's device**. It is never transmitted to our servers.

Android Auto Backup may sync this data to the user's Google account cloud backup, encrypted by Google and accessible only by the user.

## 3. Advertising

The App displays banner ads via Google AdMob. AdMob's data practices follow Google's policies: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>

Users can purchase the ad-removal in-app purchase (₩1,900) to disable ads.

## 4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share user personal data with third parties.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

## 6. User Rights

- **Export data**: Settings > Export Data (JSON) to download your data anytime.
- **Wipe data**: Settings > Wipe All Data to delete everything immediately.
- **Uninstall**: Uninstalling the App removes all local data.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will publish updates here.
